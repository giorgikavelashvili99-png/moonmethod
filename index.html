TikTok upload checklist
  • Upload in PREX Method mode (recommended) - it keeps the video 100% byte-identical (zero loss) so TikTok serves full resolution. Re-encode is the fallback only when PREX can't process the file.
  • Upload from PC (tiktok.com/upload or TikTok Studio web) - never from the phone app, it re-encodes and undoes the optimization.
  • Set post privacy to Everyone on the upload screen, then re-select "Everyone" one more time right before clicking Post - TikTok Studio web has a known bug (since 2026) that silently switches posts to "Only me" even when "Everyone" was chosen.
  • After posting, open the Check video tab and confirm the result shows Public (not an error) before moving on.
  • Do not edit / crop / add music inside TikTok after uploading - any in-app edit re-encodes the file.
  • Toggle "Allow high-quality uploads" ON in TikTok Settings → Content preferences.
  • Do not re-upload the same optimized file to multiple accounts - TikTok's dedup system links identical uploads across accounts.
  • If a post still lands on "Only me": flip the privacy back to Everyone on the post itself (app or web) - this works on TikTok's side; also clear tiktok.com cookies, re-login, and retry with a fresh session if it keeps happening.

2 · How to upload (step by step)
1 Export clean from your editor
Use the Export Settings assistant above (card 1). Always export H.264/AVC + AAC in an MP4 container at a constant frame rate (60 or 120) — variable frame rate makes TikTok's player tag drop below 1080P/60.
2 Run the Optimizer
Open the Optimizer tab, drop your export and choose PREX Method (recommended) — the video track stays 100% byte-identical while the audio sample table is exposed for full-resolution serving. Watch the Upload readiness card after it analyzes your file.
3 Upload from a PC browser
Go to tiktok.com/upload or TikTok Studio web — never the phone app, it re-encodes and undoes the optimization. Set privacy to Everyone, then re-select "Everyone" one more time right before clicking Post (TikTok Studio web has a known bug since 2026 that silently switches posts to "Only me").
4 Turn on "Allow high-quality uploads"
TikTok Settings → Content preferences → toggle "Allow high-quality uploads" ON. Without it, TikTok is far more likely to serve a lower-bitrate version of your video.
5 Verify with the Check tab
Open the Check video tab, paste the post URL and confirm it shows Public with 1080p (or your target) served — the check is now saved in Recent checks below. If it shows SD or "Only me", use the post-privacy flip + re-login fix.
