Options
Clean remux (safest, zero tricks): remuxes the file with FFmpeg (moov first, faststart, all CapCut/editor metadata stripped) WITHOUT touching the video, its timing or its audio. The upload keeps its real 60fps and normal playback speed - it is just a clean, canonical container. Best choice when you only want to avoid "Only me" and don't need extra sharpness. Note: the audio stays bit-identical to your CapCut export, so do not re-upload the same export to several accounts.
High-quality re-encode: decodes the video and re-encodes it with libx264 plus a sharpening pass - a much better "master" for TikTok's own re-encode, so the served result is noticeably sharper. Sharper = CRF 14, medium preset (best quality, now faster). Faster = CRF 18, veryfast preset (several times quicker, still sharp).
